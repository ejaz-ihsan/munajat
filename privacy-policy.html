Munajat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Munajat is a dua app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your name, daily intentions, reading progress, saved duas and settings are stored only on your own device. They never leave your phone. Deleting the app, or using "Reset All Data" in the app, removes them permanently.

Notifications

If you turn on Daily Reminders, the app asks your permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device only. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
