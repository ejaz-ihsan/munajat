Munajat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Munajat is a dua app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

When you create a Munajat account, we store your email address and your Munajat progress: your name, intentions, saved duas, checklist ticks, Arabic words learned and settings. This lets you log in and keep your progress on any phone.

The app has no advertising and no tracking. We never sell or share your information.

Where it is stored

Your account and progress are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). A copy of your progress is also kept on your device so the app works offline.

Deleting your information

"Reset All Data" in the app clears your progress. To delete your account and email completely, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

Notifications

If you turn on Daily Reminders, the app asks your permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device only. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Children

Munajat accounts are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
